2 The Bound-Setting Code (Direct Cause)

File: gams/inputs/dist_input_solve.gms, lines 33–37

v_betas.LO("EAA_REL",E_PACT,EST_I,EYEARS) $ p_supports("EAA_REL",E_PACT,EST_I,"Mean",EYEARS)
    = 0.20 * p_supports("EAA_REL",E_PACT,EST_I,"Mean",EYEARS);

v_betas.UP("EAA_REL",E_PACT,EST_I,EYEARS) $ p_supports("EAA_REL",E_PACT,EST_I,"Mean",EYEARS)
    = 5.00 * p_supports("EAA_REL",E_PACT,EST_I,"Mean",EYEARS);

This code assumes p_supports is positive:

Sign of p_supportsLO = 0.20×UP = 5.00×Result
Positivesmall positivelarge positiveLO < UP ✓
Negativesmall negative (closer to 0)large negative (farther from 0)LO > UP ✗

Numerical Example

For v_betas(EAA_REL, OFARorg, REPMorg, 2010) with p_supports Mean ≈ −10.10:

  • LO = 0.20 × (−10.10) = −2.02
  • UP = 5.00 × (−10.10) = −50.48
  • −2.02 > −50.48 → bound error

4 The Bound-Widening Loop Makes It Worse

File: gams/inputs/dist_input_solve.gms, lines 134–140

v_betas.lo(...) = v_betas.lo(...) * 0.9**p_nSolved(years);
v_betas.up(...) = v_betas.up(...) * 1.1**p_nSolved(years);

When both bounds are negative:

  • Multiplying LO (−2.02) by 0.9 → −1.82 (moves toward zero, increases LO)
  • Multiplying UP (−50.48) by 1.1 → −55.53 (moves away from zero, decreases UP)

This widens the LO > UP gap with each solve iteration.

5 Why Only Year 2010 and Only Cyprus?

Year 2010

Year 2010 is the first year in the organic solve loop where CY organic activities have non-zero levels. Years 1998–2009 are solved for conventional activities and succeed. Year 2010 triggers organic input allocation for the first time, but CY's tiny organic sector cannot sustain the expected cost allocations.

Small Member States

CY has an extremely small organic sector (activity levels of 0.004–5.6 units). The summed organic revenues (~1.23 Mio €) are insufficient to cover subtracted organic EAA sectoral costs (~2.89 Mio €).

Comparison with Austria: p_totRev(REPMorg, 2010) = +430.0 — AT's organic sector is large enough → no error.

6 Summary Diagram

CY has very small organic sector → LEVL values tiny (0.004 to 5.6) → Σ(p_expRev × LEVL)/1000 ≈ 1.23 Mio € → Subtracted EAA costs ≈ 2.89 Mio € → p_totRev(REPMorg/REPBorg/INPOorg, 2010) = −1.648 → p_expCostsFromSectorRelations = (+0.158) / (−1.648) × rev_term = NEGATIVE → p_supports("EAA_REL",...,"Mean",...) = NEGATIVE → v_betas.LO = 0.20 × negative = −2.02 (closer to 0) v_betas.UP = 5.00 × negative = −50.48 (farther from 0) → LO > UP → GAMS SOLVE ABORTED (EXECERROR=30)

9 Possible Fixes

Option A — Guard the Bounds (symptom fix)

In dist_input_solve.gms (lines 33–37), use MIN/MAX to ensure correct ordering:

v_betas.LO("EAA_REL",E_PACT,EST_I,EYEARS) $ p_supports(...)
    = MIN(0.20 * p_supports(...), 5.00 * p_supports(...));

v_betas.UP("EAA_REL",E_PACT,EST_I,EYEARS) $ p_supports(...)
    = MAX(0.20 * p_supports(...), 5.00 * p_supports(...));

Option B — Clamp p_totRev (root cause fix)

In dist_input_prep_prior.gms (after line 162):

p_totRev(est_r_org,YEARS) $ (p_totRev(est_r_org,YEARS) LT 0) = EPS;

Option C — Guard p_expCostsFromSectorRelations (intermediate fix)

In dist_input_prep_prior.gms (line 429), add condition:

... $ (p_totRev(est_r_org,YEARS) GT 0) ...

Recommendation

Option B + Option A combined: Option B prevents economically nonsensical negative total revenues at the source; Option A provides a safety net at the bounds level regardless of upstream data quality.
